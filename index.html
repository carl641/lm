The firm

Contact us today

Serving the Limestone County, Alabama area, the attorneys of Latham Moffatt, P.C. provide knowledgeable representation backed by over 75 years of combined trial and litigation experience. Our team includes the University of Alabama School of Law's 2009 Pipes Distinguished Alumnus, a mark of the standard we hold ourselves to.

As a locally owned firm in Athens, we understand the legal challenges our neighbors face. We deliver compassionate client care while advocating strongly for your rights and your interests. Our office sits a block from the courthouse, which lets us handle your matter efficiently and keep your legal expenses reasonable.

We work to keep quality legal representation within reach of everyone in this community. That means same‑day consultations when our schedule allows, and a no recovery, no fee policy for personal injury cases. As registered civil and domestic mediators, we often resolve disputes without lengthy court proceedings, saving you time and money.

We pair local values with effective legal strategy. Our attorneys stay accessible from the first meeting to the last, so you always understand where your case stands. When you hire this firm, you get attorneys who care about the outcome of your particular situation. Contact us today to talk yours through.
