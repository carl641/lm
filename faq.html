Questions, answered plainly.

The things people most often ask before they call. Your situation will have its own answers. These are the general ones.

Working with the firm

How do I get started?

Call (256) 232-2510 during office hours. Briefly describe the kind of matter you have. Our staff will confirm we can take it, then schedule a time for you to meet with an attorney.

What should I bring to a first meeting?

Anything you have: court papers, police reports, letters from lawyers or insurers, and a simple list of dates and names. If you have nothing in writing, come anyway. Your account of what happened is the most important thing.

How much will it cost?

It depends on the matter, and you will know before you commit. Fees are discussed openly at your first meeting so you can decide with the numbers in front of you.

Will my information stay private?

Yes. What you tell us in consultation is confidential, whether or not you hire us.

Do you handle cases outside Limestone County?

Yes. The firm serves Athens, Madison, and the surrounding North Alabama area. If your matter belongs with a different kind of lawyer, we will say so and help you find one.

Courts, cases, and timing

How long will my case take?

It depends on the court's calendar, the other side, and the issues in dispute. At your first meeting we will give you a realistic range for your specific matter instead of a one‑size answer.

Do I really need a lawyer?

Some matters are simple enough to handle alone, and we will tell you if yours is one of them. But court deadlines and filing rules are unforgiving. A short consultation early usually costs far less than repairing a mistake later.

What is mediation, and is it right for my dispute?

Mediation is a structured negotiation led by a neutral. It is private, usually faster than trial, and the parties keep control of the outcome. Both of our attorneys are registered civil and domestic mediators.

Can one of you be the mediator and my lawyer?

No. A mediator is a neutral and does not represent either side. We can act as your advocate in a mediation someone else leads, or as the neutral mediator in a dispute where neither party is our client.

What if something happens outside office hours?

Call the office and leave a brief message. If you are in immediate danger, call 911 first. If you are arrested, remember that you have the right to remain silent and the right to ask for a lawyer.
