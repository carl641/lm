When you call

You will reach our office staff. Describe your matter in a sentence or two. We will check for conflicts of interest, then set a time for you to meet with an attorney.

Court deadlines move quickly. If you have been served with papers or charged with a crime, call sooner rather than later.

Please keep voicemail messages brief and general. Contacting the firm does not by itself create an attorney‑client relationship.
