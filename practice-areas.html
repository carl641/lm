Family Law

When a family changes shape, the stakes are personal.

Divorce and custody cases are won with preparation and settled with judgment. We guide clients through both, protecting what matters most: children, homes, and futures.

Where agreement is possible, we pursue it. Where it is not, we try the case.

  • Divorce
  • Child custody and visitation
  • Child support
  • Spousal support
  • Protection from abuse orders
  • Modifications and enforcement

Criminal Defense

A charge is not a conviction. What you do next matters.

From a first DUI to serious felony charges, we defend clients in the courts of Limestone County and North Alabama. Early counsel shapes everything that follows: what you say, what gets filed, and how your case resolves.

  • Felony and misdemeanor defense
  • DUI defense
  • Traffic violations
  • Bond and first appearances
  • Probation matters
  • Trial defense

Estate Planning

Your wishes, written so they hold.

A clear estate plan spares your family confusion and conflict. We draft wills, trusts, and powers of attorney matched to your circumstances, and keep them current as life changes.

  • Wills
  • Trusts
  • Powers of attorney
  • Advance directives
  • Beneficiary planning
  • Plan reviews and updates

Probate

Settling an estate, without adding to the grief.

Probate mixes paperwork, deadlines, and family in a difficult season. We guide executors and heirs through the Limestone County probate process, from first filing to final settlement.

  • Probating wills
  • Estate administration
  • Intestate estates
  • Executor guidance
  • Creditor claims
  • Estate disputes

Personal Injury

When someone else's negligence changes your life.

After a serious injury, insurance companies move quickly, and not in your favor. We deal with them so you can focus on recovery, and we prepare every claim as if it will be tried.

  • Car and truck accidents
  • Serious injury claims
  • Insurance negotiations
  • Litigation and trial

Mediation

A settled dispute is often the best verdict.

Both of our attorneys are registered civil and domestic mediators. As neutrals, they help opposing parties reach agreements a court would take months to impose, privately and often at far less cost.

A mediator is neutral and does not represent either side. We can serve as the neutral in your dispute, or as your advocate in a mediation led by someone else.

  • Civil mediation
  • Domestic and family mediation
  • Pre‑suit mediation
  • Court‑referred mediation
