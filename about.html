A firm built on the square.

Latham Moffatt, P.C. is a Limestone County law firm through and through: local clients, local courts, and an office one block from the courthouse.

Sixty years, one square.

Byrd Latham began practicing law in Athens in 1964, the year he finished the University of Alabama School of Law. Jim Moffatt joined the Limestone County bar in 1982. In 2004 they formed Latham Moffatt, P.C., the professional corporation that carries their names today.

The work has stayed close to home ever since: family law, criminal defense, estate planning, probate, and personal injury for the people of Athens, Madison, and the surrounding counties. Between them, our attorneys bring more than 75 years of trial and litigation experience to every matter they take.

Both attorneys are registered civil and domestic mediators, and the firm holds Martindale‑Hubbell's AV Preeminent peer rating, the directory's highest rating for legal ability and ethical standards.
