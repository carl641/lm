The attorneys.

Two lawyers, one standard: prepared, plainspoken, and answerable to their neighbors.

Byrd R. Latham

Attorney and registered mediator

Byrd Latham received his law degree from the University of Alabama in 1964 and has practiced in Limestone County ever since. Across six decades he has handled civil litigation, corporate, probate, and domestic matters for generations of North Alabama families.

In 2009 the University of Alabama School of Law named him its Pipes Distinguished Alumnus, recognizing a career of service to the profession and the community.

  • EducationJ.D., University of Alabama School of Law, 1964
  • BarAlabama State Bar
  • PracticeCivil litigation, corporate, probate, and domestic relations
  • MediationRegistered civil and domestic mediator
  • RecognitionPipes Distinguished Alumnus, University of Alabama School of Law, 2009

James D. Moffatt

Attorney and registered mediator

Jim Moffatt earned his law degree from the University of Alabama in 1982 and has practiced in Limestone County for more than four decades, trying and settling cases for families and businesses across North Alabama.

Like his partner, he is a registered civil and domestic mediator, helping opposing parties resolve disputes without the cost and strain of a trial.

  • EducationJ.D., University of Alabama School of Law, 1982
  • BarAlabama State Bar
  • PracticeTrial and settlement work across the firm's practice areas
  • MediationRegistered civil and domestic mediator
